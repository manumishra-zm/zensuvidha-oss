System topology

three tiers · one process

PLATE 02

Signal chain — one turn

wide path = common case

Everything indented is an exception that fires only when its condition holds. Four gates keep the ordinary turn cheap; the expensive stages are reached rarely and on purpose.

CapturegetUserMedia

Echo cancellation and noise suppression on, automatic gain off — AGC rescales the very signal the VAD's noise floor adapts to.

Band splitAudioWorklet off the main thread

Raw PCM goes to the server untouched; a 2-pole biquad at 300–3400 Hz feeds only the level gate — 2.79× noise rejection. Whisper never sees band-limited audio.

Voice activitySilero v5

Answers is this speech? — never whose?. Requires the previous frame's last 64 samples prepended, or it scores 0.11 on clear speech instead of 0.99 — and looks healthy doing it.

Endpoint decisionlearns per caller
short answer→ 800 ms — haan, a phone number
> 1.5 s→ 1200 ms — long enough to have a middle
learned→ up to 2000 ms, from turns we demonstrably cut
sounds unfinished→ +900 ms — the words say so, not the silence
Latch guardcontinuous audio discards the turn

Seven seconds recorded and never once a 260 ms pause? People breathe; a speaker at full volume does not. Throw it away and ask them to repeat, rather than handing Whisper fifteen seconds of noise and receiving an empty string.

Self-echo guardbarge-in

Interrupting also requires the audio to be louder than a room-attenuated echo of our own output. The browser's AEC hides this today — a telephony transport has no getUserMedia, and the agent would interrupt itself on a loop.

Speaker isolationpipeline.prepare

Runs on raw audio: identity is measurably worse on denoised audio (0.675 vs 0.596), and this is the only stage that can remove another human voice.

Across 30 measured conditions it won one and lost eight. It costs accuracy as well as 226–475 ms — see Plate 05.

Ships inside faster-whisper. This is what stops the recogniser inventing 5,5,5,5… out of noise.

PLATE 03

Identity — when the gate may refuse

two independent conditions

This component has caused more user-visible failures than anything else in the system, and every repair has moved it toward refusing less. One measurement explains all of them.

The finding The acceptance threshold was calibrated on synthetic audio.
tested onsame speakerat 0.55
macOS say voices0.867separates them
a real microphone0.27–0.41refuses the caller
In a room the noise router measured clean, a caller's own consecutive turns scored 0.37 / 0.27 / 0.29 / 0.41 — never once above the threshold. The gate could not recognise them at all, so every refusal it made was noise.

Two independent conditions must therefore hold before anybody is refused:

  • Proven — it has matched this caller at least once on this call. A gate that has never recognised them has no evidence it can, and refusing on that is a coin toss that silences whoever is actually on the phone.
  • Corroborated — the voiceprint is more than one un-corroborated sample.

While either is missing, a mismatch is answered and the voice remembered as a rival. A rival that returns twice becomes the caller: the voice having the conversation is the caller, and a song does not follow up on its own question.

Root cause of two lockouts Our own trimming was refusing the caller. _voiced_only spliced out the gaps between words, and every join is an artificial transient the encoder hears as part of the voice. It scored the caller 0.450 against their own sentence — below threshold. Keeping the voiced span gives 0.843. The tell was a metric that got worse with more audio: 3.0 s → 0.507 against 2.0 s → 0.818.

PLATE 05

Operating envelope

measured, by interference type

The right-hand column is the caller-relative signal-to-noise ratio at which the turn still works — words intact and the caller still recognised. −6 dB means it survives interference twice as loud as the caller; +12 dB means the caller must be four times louder than the interference. The bar is that headroom drawn out: longer is more tolerant.

InterferenceNoise tolerance Works to
mains hum 50 Hz −6 dB
traffic rumble −6 dB
music, no vocals −6 dB
another speaker 0 dB
pink / room tone +3 dB
music with vocals +3 dB
TV / babble +3 dB
white noise +6 dB
fan · air-conditioner +12 dB
least tolerantmost tolerant

Bar length is linear in dB across an 18 dB span; the colour band is the verdict, not a second scale.

Two patterns. Low-frequency and tonal interference is nearly free — hum, rumble and instrumental music all survive to −6 dB with words intact. Broadband interference is expensive, and a ceiling fan is the worst case in the set: the caller must be four times louder than it. And anything containing a human voice takes out the speaker gate before it takes out the transcript — the words usually survive; recognising them as yours is what fails.

Why auto-denoise ships off DeepFilterNet, swept across six interference types at five signal-to-noise ratios — thirty conditions. It won 1 cell, lost 8 and tied 21, for a net −400 % word recall. Where it lost, it lost badly:
conditionword recall
white hiss−50 %
fan · air-conditioner−67 %
music with vocals−100 %
It is also the most expensive stage in the pipeline at 226–475 ms. On the recognition path it costs time and accuracy — the same verdict noisereduce received. Whisper trained on 680,000 hours of messy audio; it does not need the help. The manual toggle remains for A/B comparison.

Latency budget

M1 Pro · CPU · warm

Measured end to end with the models warm, which is the state a real call is in from turn two onward.

Stage3 s clip6 s clipNotes
Isolation66 ms146 ms diarization + ECAPA. ~6 % of the path.
Speaker gate34 ms54 ms one embedding, reused by the rejection path.
Recognition1070 ms1168 ms the dominant cost.
LLM first token400–600 ms— English. Hindi 3535 ms, Telugu 5299 ms.
Synthesis385 ms— first sentence; the rest overlaps playback.

The audio path is recognition-bound. Four attempts to speed isolation further were measured and rejected: ECAPA on Metal fails outright (SpeechBrain retains internal CPU tensors); batching the thirds gate is a real 1.5× on a 60 ms stage; cpu_threads is worse with more threads on Apple silicon (auto 1691 · 8 threads 2212 · 10 threads 3798 ms); and fast-decode mode is not reliably faster.

The Indic ceiling Latency in Indian languages is tokenizer inflation, not runtime. At 38.7 tok/s the same forty-token reply is 1.03 s in English, 3.62 s in Hindi and 6.41 s in Telugu. No inference library changes that — which is why MLX was benchmarked and rejected. A GPU is the answer, and it also restores the five languages that currently have no voice at all.
PLATE 07

Three designs that look wasteful

and are load-bearing

Each of these looks like an obvious saving on a first reading of the code. Each was tried and put back.

the 6,089-token prompt It carries weight twice. The entire knowledge base sits in a byte-identical prefix so Ollama reuses its KV cache — which is what makes a steady-state turn 640 ms — and the full English knowledge base is what lets Hindi and Telugu callers get answers at all, because English tags cannot retrieve against a native-script query. Trimming it breaks both at once.
Silero running twice Version 5 in the browser answers should I open a turn?; version 6 on the server answers what should Whisper see?. Different questions — and the second copy ships free inside faster-whisper.
two protocol messages that do nothing on a good call commit_miss and turn_dropped exist so a call can never wedge. The client enters "thinking" the instant it ships audio and only leaves on a reply, so every path that declines to answer must say so. Silence is often the right reply; it is never the right protocol.
PLATE 08

Known limits

stated, not hidden
  • Fan and air-conditioner noise needs +12 dB. The caller must be four times louder than a ceiling fan — the commonest condition in an Indian small business — and denoising cannot rescue it (measured −67 %).
  • Five languages have no voice on macOS: Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia and Urdu. Those callers see the reply and hear nothing. Warned at startup; fixed only by the GPU preset with Indic-Parler.
  • Loud audio at the microphone destroys the voiceprint (0.07 against the caller's own voice). Mitigated by the latch guard, which cuts the buffer and asks for a repeat. Not solved.
  • Echo cancellation depends on the browser. All AEC comes from getUserMedia; a telephony transport has none. A level-based guard is in place, but real server-side AEC is required before a phone line.
  • Every remaining threshold is calibrated on synthetic speech. A real microphone has already proved one of them wrong by more than two-fold. Real recordings are the single highest-value missing input to this project.
  • No LICENSE file, and XTTS — a selectable provider — is non-commercial and undocumented as such.
